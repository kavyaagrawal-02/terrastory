Kavya Agrawal

Founder, geographer and cartographer

Kavya founded Terrastory to bring the precision of geographic research to maps that people actually use: gifts, guest maps, land reports and research figures.

27.18°N 78.01°E Agra

Background

Kavya is a geographer trained in remote sensing, GIS and environmental data, with a lifelong love of maps.

Her doctoral research at TERI School of Advanced Studies, New Delhi, uses satellite data, GIS and machine learning to predict groundwater contamination across India. That work means reading landscapes from space every day: terrain, water, vegetation and how places change over time.

Terrastory brings the same care to every map, whether it's a village for a family gift or a study area for a journal paper.
