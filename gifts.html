Questions

Can you map a small village that barely shows on Google Maps?

Yes. We work from satellite imagery, so even small villages, farms and lanes can be mapped. Send a Google Maps pin and tell us what matters there.

Can I add names, a date or a message?

Yes. Most gifts carry a title, a short message, a date and the coordinates of the place. Tell us the exact text when you order.

Will I see it before it's final?

Yes. You get a watermarked preview first, and two rounds of changes are included.

How fast can I get a Map Greeting?

Usually the same day, if we have your details and payment by the afternoon. Festival weeks are busy, so order early.

Do you print and ship?

Yes, Hometown & Memory Maps can be printed and shipped across India. The print size is confirmed when you order.

Which place should we map?

Send a pin. We'll take it from there.
