Questions

Is this a legal or survey document?

No. It's a geographic screening based on satellite and public data. It is not title verification, a boundary survey, a valuation, an engineering assessment or a statutory approval.

Do I need the exact plot boundary?

No. A Google Maps pin is enough. A rough boundary, a sketch or the plot size helps us mark it more clearly.

How far back can you go?

Usually to around 2000, using freely available satellite archives. Older or very detailed imagery depends on what exists for that area.

Can it show flooding or waterlogging?

It can show where water has appeared in past satellite images and how low-lying the area is. That's useful screening, not a flood certificate.

Can I use this to check land in my village from abroad?

Yes. Many people use it to see how family or inherited land and its surroundings have changed, without travelling.
