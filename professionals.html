Questions

Can these maps go into an EIA report?

Yes. We prepare study-area and baseline maps with sources, dates and scales stated, ready to include in your report. Final responsibility for the report stays with the accredited consultant.

How do you charge?

Professional work is quoted per project. Payment is 50% in advance and 50% on delivery.

Do you sign confidentiality agreements?

Yes. Project data is never shared, and we can sign your NDA before you send anything.

Can you deliver GIS files as well as images?

Yes. Along with PDFs and images, we can deliver shapefiles or GeoPackages where the project needs them.

Are these survey-grade maps?

No. They're built from satellite and public data for planning, reporting and presentation. Where survey-grade accuracy is required, use a licensed surveyor.
