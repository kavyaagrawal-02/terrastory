Questions

What do you need from us?

Your property's location, the places you recommend to guests, and your logo and colours. Photos of the places help but aren't required.

Can you include our own property layout?

Yes. We can draw your cottages, pool, restaurant and paths from satellite imagery and a simple sketch or plan you share.

How accurate is it?

The base is built from satellite imagery and OpenStreetMap, and every place on the map is checked with you before final delivery.

What formats do we get?

Files sized for your website, Instagram posts and stories, printable room cards and a reception poster. Tell us if you need another size.

Can the map be updated later?

Yes. If a café closes or you open a new trail, updates can be quoted separately.
