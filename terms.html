What our maps are, and are not

Our maps and reports are made for information and design purposes from satellite imagery and public geographic data. They are not legal, survey, engineering or valuation documents. A Plot Through Time report is a geographic screening. It is not title verification, a boundary survey or a statutory approval.

Maps may contain modified Copernicus Sentinel data, Landsat imagery courtesy of USGS, and map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. India boundaries are shown as per the Survey of India.
