FAQ

Do I need to know anything about maps or GIS?

No. Just send a location and tell us what it's for.

What do I need to send?

A Google Maps pin or an address. For plots, a rough boundary helps.

How do I pay?

By UPI. Gifts and plot reports are paid upfront. Business projects are 50% in advance and 50% on delivery.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included.

Can you ship prints?

Yes, across India. Shipping is charged extra.

Is a Plot Through Time report a legal or survey document?

No. It's a geographic screening based on satellite and public data. It is not title verification, valuation, an engineering assessment or a statutory approval.

Can I ask for something that isn't listed?

Yes. Message us with your place and your question.
