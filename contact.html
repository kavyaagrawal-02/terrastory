What to send us

The more we know about the place and its purpose, the better the map.

  • A Google Maps pin, an address or coordinates
  • What the map is for: a gift, a property, a plot or a project
  • Any text for the map: names, a title, a date or a message
  • Your deadline, and whether you need a print
  • For plots: a rough boundary or the plot size
  • For research: your study area and journal guidelines
