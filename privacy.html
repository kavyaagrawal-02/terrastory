Order a Map

Last updated 30 October 2026

Privacy

Terrastory is a map studio. We collect only what you send us, and only so we can make your map.

What we collect

  • Your name and how to reach you (WhatsApp number, email).
  • The place you want mapped: a Google Maps pin, address, coordinates or plot boundary.
  • Anything you choose to add to the map, such as names, dates, a message or photos.
  • Payment confirmation details, such as a UPI transaction reference. We never see or store your bank or card details.

How we use it

To make, deliver and support your order, and to reply to your questions. Nothing else.

What we never do

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it's kept

Order details live in the tools we use to run orders: Google Forms and Google Drive, WhatsApp, and email. These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your information

Ask us at any time and we'll delete your details and files. We may keep a basic record of the payment where the law requires it for accounts.

Your map in our portfolio

We may show finished maps in our portfolio. We never show your name, phone number or exact home address, and we'll leave your map out entirely if you ask us to.

This website

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser remembers only your day/night map choice. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. If we add visitor statistics, we'll use a cookie-free tool that counts visits without identifying you.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp or email us using the details on our contact page.
